We are a semiconductor engineering company. We help chip and embedded teams verify and validate their designs so they ship correct and ready to tape out.

## What we do

- **RISC-V verification:** ISA compliance, ACT4 certification, and processor validation
- **Functional verification:** SystemVerilog and UVM testbench development, from bring-up to sign-off
- **Coverage:** functional coverage modeling and coverage closure
- **Formal verification:** property checking and assertion-based verification
- **RTL and digital design:** micro-architecture and implementation
- **CDC verification:** clock-domain-crossing analysis and closure

## Who we work with

Semiconductor startups, RISC-V ecosystem companies, IP developers, and in-house silicon teams that need verification depth without the overhead of scaling a full team.
